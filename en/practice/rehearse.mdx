---
title: "Plan an interview practice session"
description: "Use a practice call or text questions to study and rehearse your answers."
---

Practice with a partner or type questions, study the suggested structure, and answer aloud in your own words.

## Prepare

Pick a theme such as introductions, project reviews, prioritization, or analysis. Select the relevant resume and role, and add project data to the Knowledge Base if useful. Set one concrete goal, such as “Explain a project in two minutes and answer two follow-ups.”

## Practice with a partner

1. Start a call in your meeting app and invite a partner to interview you.
2. Check microphone and meeting audio, then launch the assistant.
3. Have your partner ask one question at a time; organize and speak your answer first.
4. Use automatic answering or **AI Answer** for structural suggestions as needed.
5. Try one or two follow-ups on the same question.
6. Select **End Interview** and review the summary, Q&A, and transcript.

## Text questions and spoken rehearsal

Open **Chat**, type a question, and identify the conclusion, evidence, and examples in the suggestion. Look away and repeat it in your own words, using your actual responsibilities, actions, and results.

Try a basic question (“Describe your most representative project”), an evidence question (“How do you know the improvement came from your solution?”), and a trade-off (“What would you change with half the resources?”).

## Review

First check whether you answered the question, then assess structure, facts, and delivery. Choose one improvement and answer the same question again. Practice sessions use your account access too.
