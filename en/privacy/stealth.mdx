---
title: "Stealth Mode and window display"
description: "Control assistant display during sharing and check the actual shared view."
---

**Stealth Mode** in the assistant menu controls its display during screen sharing or recording.

## Check and change

1. Open **Menu** after launching the assistant.
2. Check the **Stealth Mode** switch.
3. Set it for your presentation or practice needs.
4. Check the actual shared view in the meeting software you plan to use.

Use the same devices, meeting app, and sharing method in a practice call. A second device can show what the other person receives. Recheck after changing devices, software, or sharing method.

## Related display settings

**Stealth Mode** controls assistant visibility during sharing or recording; **Collapse to Floating Bubble** shrinks the local assistant. If your client provides an app icon visibility option, adjust it separately in App Settings.

Hiding or collapsing does not stop the app. Use **End Interview** when finished.
