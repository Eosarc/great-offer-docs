Great Offer AI processes resumes, role descriptions, documents, questions, audio, and screenshots according to the features you use, to provide transcription, suggestions, and review.

## Cloud processing and records

Live transcription and AI answers require an internet connection. Relevant audio, text, or screenshots are sent to servers and the AI or transcription providers used to process them.

After you end a session and synchronization completes, transcripts, Q&A, and summaries are stored in the cloud and can be viewed or deleted in **Interview Records**. Under the Privacy Policy, raw audio is processed temporarily and is not kept as server-side audio history. Original audio playback is not currently available.

The desktop app also stores local data for session recovery and sync retries. Deleting a cloud interview record does not clear all local device data. Contact privacy support for further deletion help.

## Management options

| Content | Where |
|---|---|
| Resume, roles, documents | Corresponding workspace lists |
| Q&A, transcripts, summaries | Interview Records |
| Microphone, audio, screen access | App and operating system permission settings |
| Feedback and optional diagnostics | App settings → Help and feedback |
| Account deletion | Account & Billing → Privacy → Delete Account |

## Materials and screenshots

Only submit relevant material. Remove unrelated identifiers, passwords, payment details, and private information. Keep screenshots limited to the necessary problem context.

## Optional diagnostic logs

**Include diagnostic logs (optional)** is off by default when sending feedback. If enabled, recent app logs, permission status, and environment details are submitted and may include conversation text. Enable it only if you agree to provide that information.

## Delete your account

Open **Delete Account**, read the permanent deletion notice, and enter the required confirmation. This cannot be undone, so check materials you need to retain first. Account deletion does not mean all data is removed immediately; legally required transaction records and other retention follow the Privacy Policy.

## Policy and contact

Open **Privacy Policy** from the [website](https://greatoffer.ai/) footer for providers, retention, and rights. Contact [privacy@greatoffer.ai](mailto:privacy@greatoffer.ai) for data requests.
