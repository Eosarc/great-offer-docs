The desktop app is the entry point for the live assistant. Use its workspace to prepare materials, launch sessions, and review interview records.

## Installation and sign-in

1. Visit the [Great Offer AI website](https://greatoffer.ai/) and open the download page.
2. Choose the installer for your computer and follow the installation instructions.
3. Open Great Offer AI and sign in.
4. If sign-in opens a browser, complete the flow and return to the app as prompted.
5. Check the account at the lower left. Use the same account used to purchase your plan or minutes.

## Workspace navigation

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Home | Preparation guidance, target roles, recent records, and assistant launch |
| Interview Records | Session status, duration, and reports |
| Resume | Upload, enter, preview, and edit resumes |
| Target Roles | Save role titles, requirements, and companies |
| Knowledge Base | Manage project details, company research, and reference documents |
| Quick Start | In-app preparation and launch guidance |
| Subscription & Credits | Trials, subscription plans, and credits |
| App settings | Permissions, devices, language, and version |

Keep an internet connection available, prepare a resume and job description, check device permissions, and confirm that a trial, subscription, or credit balance is available. Current system requirements and installers are listed on the website.
