## In-app feedback

<Frame caption="Select 1 Settings, then 2 Send feedback.">
  <img src="/images/client/support-feedback-entry.png" alt="Desktop settings with step 1 pointing to Settings in the sidebar and step 2 to Send feedback under Help and Feedback." width="658" />
</Frame>

1. Open **Settings → Help and Feedback → Send feedback**, or **Feedback** in the account menu.
2. Explain the task, steps, and time in **What happened?**.
3. Add JPG, PNG, or WebP screenshots if needed: up to six, each at most 2 MB.
4. Optionally enable **Include Diagnostic Logs (Optional)**.
5. Review and select **Submit feedback**.

<Frame caption="Describe your suggestion or issue, then add screenshots and logs if needed.">
  <img src="/images/client/support-feedback-form.png" alt="Desktop Send Feedback form with an English product suggestion, screenshot upload area, unchecked diagnostic logs option, and Submit feedback button." width="520" />
</Frame>

Logs are off by default. If enabled, recent app logs, permission status, and environment details are attached and may include conversation text. Confirm you agree to provide this information. See [Privacy and data](/en/privacy/data).

## Useful details

Include the feature and intended task, time, app and operating system versions, audio devices, related session or order details, steps already tried, and relevant screenshots. Mask private information; do not send passwords, verification codes, or full payment card details.
