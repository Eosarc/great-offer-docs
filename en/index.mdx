Great Offer AI brings interview materials, a live assistant, and session records into one workspace. Prepare your resume and target role, choose a model and answer style, follow transcripts and suggestions, and review your summary, Q&A, and transcript afterward.

## Get started

1. [Install and sign in](/en/getting-started/install-and-sign-in).
2. [Check microphone, system audio, and screen permissions](/en/setup/permissions).
3. [Add a resume](/en/preparation/resumes) and [target role](/en/preparation/jobs).
4. [Configure and launch the assistant](/en/launch/configuration).
5. Ask questions through [text chat](/en/assistant/text-chat) or audio.
6. [End the interview](/en/assistant/end-session) and [review the record](/en/review/records).

For the complete walkthrough, see [Your first interview session](/en/quickstart).
