After ending, open **Interview Records → View Report → Interview summary**.

## Four parts

| Section | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Interview overview | Topics, background, and experience covered |
| Strengths | Answers that showed skills or clear communication |
| Areas to improve | Details needing more explanation or evidence |
| Improvement suggestions | Specific next actions |

Start with the overview, keep effective approaches from the strengths, then choose one suggestion and locate its question in Q&A and the transcript.

For example, if project results need clarity, check the metric definition, baseline, outcome, and observation period. Rehearse again with the missing evidence.

## Check against the original content

Treat the summary as feedback for reflection. Verify facts, figures, and personal experience against the transcript and your materials. If few spoken answers were captured, the summary may have limited material to evaluate. Answer fully and check your transcript in the next practice.
