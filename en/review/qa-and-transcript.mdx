Open **Interview Records → View Report** and select **Q&A** or **Transcript**.

## Q&A: questions and suggestions

Read each question and its AI answer. Use the question navigation to move through long records. Identify what was asked, the suggested structure, what fits your experience, and what needs more detail. Turn useful conclusions into your own outline.

## Transcript: the actual conversation

Review text by speaker and time to recall questions, your spoken answers, and context. Verify numbers, terminology, and project names against your materials. Scroll through long records and use **Back to top** when needed.

## Compare effectively

1. Find the original question and your answer in the transcript.
2. Write your actual conclusion in one sentence.
3. Read the AI suggestion and select useful structure or evidence.
4. Make three to five points and answer aloud again.

Reading AI suggestions and your own spoken answers separately helps reveal the practice needed between understanding an idea and explaining it clearly.
