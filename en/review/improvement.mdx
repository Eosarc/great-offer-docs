A useful review ends with a specific next practice task.

| Focus | Check | Example task |
|---|---|---|
| Structure | Conclusion first and relevant detail | Answer with conclusion, evidence, example |
| Facts | Clear responsibilities, actions, results | Fill in project roles and figures |
| Reasoning | Explain trade-offs and attribution | Prepare arguments for and against one choice |
| Delivery | Concise, natural spoken language | Reduce a long answer to one minute |

## A simple note

> Question: How do you know your solution caused the activation increase?
>
> Improvement: Explain the experiment design, rather than just results.
>
> Missing details: Assignment, sample size, period, and guardrail metrics.
>
> Next practice: Explain in two minutes, then answer one attribution follow-up.

## Start another round

Update facts in your resume or Knowledge Base, choose the relevant role, and launch a new session. Practice the same question and compare transcripts: did you state the conclusion earlier, add evidence, and reduce digressions?

Keep one main goal per round. Add English delivery, time limits, or consecutive follow-ups once it becomes consistent.
