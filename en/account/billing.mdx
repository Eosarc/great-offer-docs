Before changing paid access, check your current plan, paying account, and billing period. **Subscription & Credits** also provides plan and payment FAQs.

## Automatic renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically for the selected cycle until canceled. Time Packs are one-time purchases without automatic renewal.

To avoid the next renewal, cancel through account plan management at least **24 hours before renewal**, and confirm the result. Current access usually continues until the billing period ends; check status, expiry date, and effective time.

Cancellation and refunds are handled separately. Review the refund policy and submit a request if needed.

## Plans and payment methods

Open **Account → My Plan** or **Subscription & Credits**. Check the amount, billing period, and effective arrangement before changing plans. Available payment methods appear at checkout.

## Refund conditions

Open **Refund Policy** from the [website](https://greatoffer.ai/) footer for terms applying to your product, purchase date, and usage. Eligibility, deadlines, fees, and processing times follow that policy and the order terms.

Contact [support@greatoffer.ai](mailto:support@greatoffer.ai) with your registered email, order or charge date, product, reason, and relevant supporting information.
