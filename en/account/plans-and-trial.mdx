Open **Subscription & Credits** to view options available to your account.

| Type | How it works |
|---|---|
| Free trial | Eligible new accounts can claim once; check the page for duration and eligibility |
| Subscription | Monthly, quarterly, or annual billing with access for the selected period |
| Time Pack | One-time purchase of minutes; no automatic renewal, and minutes never expire |

Check for a trial entry before your first use. Claimed trials show their status.

## Choose a plan

Consider a Time Pack for irregular practice and a subscription for preparing for several interviews over a period. Compare access, billing cycle, offers, checkout total, and renewal terms. Read quarterly or annual monthly-equivalent prices alongside the full cycle payment.

## Before and after purchase

Confirm the offer and checkout details. After payment, check your plan or minutes in the account and the entry in purchase history.

Prices, currency, offers, and eligibility can vary by account and promotion. Refer to **Subscription & Credits** and final checkout.
