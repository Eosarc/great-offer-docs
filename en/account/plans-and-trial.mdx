Open **Subscription & Credits**. Use **Credits** for one-time purchases and **Subscription** for subscription plans and the free trial.

| Type | How it works |
|---|---|
| Free trial | New users get a one-time 10-minute free trial, no credit card required |
| Subscription | Monthly, quarterly, or annual billing with access for the selected period |
| Credits | One-time purchase; 1 credit equals 1 minute of interview time, with no automatic renewal or expiry |

## Choose a plan

Consider credits for irregular practice and a subscription for preparing for several interviews over a period. Compare benefits, billing cycle, offers, checkout total, and renewal terms. Read quarterly or annual monthly-equivalent prices alongside the full cycle payment.

Check the total credits included in a purchase, including any bonus shown on the page. Prices, currency, offers, and trial eligibility follow the options shown for your account and final checkout.

## After purchase

Open **Account & Billing** to check your subscription or credits, and **View usage history** to review records. If benefits have not arrived, return to Dashboard and follow any order-status verification prompt. Contact support with your order number if they are still missing.
