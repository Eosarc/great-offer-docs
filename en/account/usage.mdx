Open the account menu at the lower left and select **Account** to see email, linked accounts, current plan, and Time Packs.

## Remaining access

Check **My Plan** and the remaining and used minutes under **Time Pack**. **View All Plans** opens available options. The account card also summarizes access before launch.

## Usage and purchases

Select **View Details** in the Time Pack area to open **Usage & Purchase History**. Usage shows time, session, duration, charge, and remaining balance; purchase history shows date, product, amount, and minutes.

Time Packs accumulate actual seconds per session and round up to a whole minute. For example, 1 minute 05 seconds deducts 2 minutes.

Waiting, reading, answering, or temporarily leaving can all happen within an active session. Finish with **End Interview**, then check the account ledger for charges.

## Account checks

Use the same email and linked sign-in method as your purchase. For help, send support the account email, purchase date, order details, and relevant session time.
