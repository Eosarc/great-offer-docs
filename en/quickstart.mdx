Use this guide during a practice call to learn the full workflow: prepare, configure, ask, end, and review.

## 1. Check devices and account access

Open **App Settings** to check microphone, system audio, and screen recording permissions. Confirm interviewer audio and your microphone, then check the account card or **Subscription & Credits** for available access.

## 2. Add a resume

Open **Resumes → Upload Resume**. Give it a recognizable title and upload a PDF, DOCX, TXT, or MD file up to 5 MB. Alternatively, switch to manual entry and paste the text. Preview the saved resume and check experience, projects, dates, and figures.

## 3. Add a target role

Open **Target Roles → Add Role**, enter the role title and requirements, and optionally add the company. Paste the responsibilities and requirements from the job listing, then save. Upload project background or company research to the **Knowledge Base** if needed.

## 4. Configure and launch

Open **Home → Launch Assistant**. Choose a resume, role, AI model, optional document, and answer language. Confirm automatic answering and answer preferences, then select **Start Interview**.

The assistant contains a transcript area, answer area, and toolbar with **AI Answer**, **Screenshot Analysis**, **Chat**, and **Audio Settings**.

## 5. Ask a question

Open **Chat**, enter “Give me three points for introducing a user activation project in a product manager interview,” and send it. Your question and AI suggestion appear in the answer area.

For audio practice, check interviewer audio and your microphone in **Audio Settings**. Automatic answering responds to detected questions; **AI Answer** lets you trigger a suggestion yourself. Read the suggestion, then answer aloud in your own words to leave material for review.

## 6. End and review

Select **Interview Duration → End Interview**. After returning to the workspace, open the record and review:

- **Interview Summary**: overview, strengths, areas to improve, and suggestions.
- **Q&A**: questions and AI answers.
- **Transcript**: conversation text, speakers, and timestamps.

Collapsing the assistant or quitting the app can leave the session running. Use **End Interview** when finished.
