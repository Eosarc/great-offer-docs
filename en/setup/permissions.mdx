---
title: "Check device permissions"
description: "Prepare permissions for your microphone, meeting audio, and screenshots."
---

Open **App Settings → Interview Access Permissions** to view the permissions required on your device and their status. The items shown depend on your client.

## What permissions do

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Microphone | Capture your voice for transcription and review |
| System audio | Capture interviewer audio from meeting software |
| Screen access | Capture the screen for screenshot analysis; some systems also require it for audio capture |

Your microphone and system audio are separate sources. Check both your voice and the meeting audio.

## Check and grant permissions

1. Check permission status in Great Offer AI **App Settings**.
2. For an item that needs access, select its permission or settings control.
3. Follow the app and operating system prompts to allow access for Great Offer AI. If you open system settings, return to the app afterward.
4. Return and check the status. If a restart is required, end any active session before restarting the app.

Permission names, settings locations, and authorization steps vary by operating system and version. Follow the prompts on your device.

## Before a session

Choose interviewer audio and microphone devices in **App Settings**. After connecting headphones or external devices, confirm that the meeting software and assistant use the intended devices. Check both voices in a practice call.
