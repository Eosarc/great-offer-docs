---
title: "Configure and launch the assistant"
description: "Choose the materials, model, language, and answer style for a session."
---

Select **Launch Assistant** on Home. Have your resume, target role, device permissions, and account access ready.

## Check each setting

| Setting | Selection |
|---|---|
| Resume | Required; choose the version for this role |
| Role | Required; check title and company |
| AI model | Choose from the current list |
| Document (optional) | Select a relevant Knowledge Base document or none |
| Interview language | Choose the AI answer output language or Auto |
| Automatic answering | Decide whether to detect questions and answer automatically |
| Answer preferences | Choose format, length, and tone |

<Frame caption="Choose your materials and answer settings before starting.">

</Frame>

Select **Start Interview** and wait for the assistant. Check devices and connection status in **Audio Settings**.

## Suggested first setup

Start with familiar materials and **Answer + key points / Medium / Natural**. Try a specific text question to learn the answer area.

To control timing yourself, turn off automatic answering before launch, then use **AI Answer** or **Chat** in the session.

## Next time

Check the displayed materials and preferences before each launch. Update workspace materials when your interview focus changes. Finish with **Interview Duration → End Interview**.
