title: "Set answer preferences"
description: "Adjust format, length, and tone for easier reading and speaking."
---

Open **Home → Launch Copilot → Answer preferences**. Expand the panel and use **Answer preview** to see structural changes.

## Answer format

| Option | Use |
|---|---|
| Full answer | Read a connected response |
| Answer + key points | Read the reasoning, then identify the main points |
| Key points | Scan quickly and use your own wording |

## Length and tone

Choose **Short**, **Balanced**, or **Detailed**. Short works well for familiar questions; Detailed helps you study the reasoning. Choose **Natural** or **Formal** to match the situation and your speaking style.

## Apply your preferences

1. Expand **Answer preferences**.
2. Choose format, length, and tone.
3. Check **Answer preview** for readability.
4. Return to the launch settings and confirm the summary before starting.

The preview uses a fixed example. Actual suggestions depend on your questions and selected materials.

Try **Answer + key points / Balanced / Natural** for your first practice, then adjust for reading speed. Practice turning key points into your own spoken answer.
