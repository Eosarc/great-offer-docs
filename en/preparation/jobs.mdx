title: "Add and manage target roles"

---

Open **Target Roles** from the sidebar or the role area on Home.

## Add a role

1. Select **Add Role**.
2. Enter a **Role name**, such as “Senior Product Manager.”
3. Paste the job description into **Role requirements**, keeping responsibilities, qualifications, and interview priorities.
4. Add the **Company (optional)** if needed.
5. Select **Save**. The role appears in the list and on Home.

## Organize requirements

Prioritize the business or product, problems to solve, skills, experience level, collaborators, and seniority. Remove recruiting copy that does not help preparation.

> Own enterprise customer activation and retention, define funnel metrics, and drive experiments. Bring B2B SaaS experience and work with engineering, design, sales, and customer success. Interview priorities include trade-offs, analysis, and collaboration.

## Reuse and update

Select **Preview** to read details or **Edit** to update the requirements or company. Save separate roles when companies or seniority levels have substantially different requirements, then choose the right one when launching.
