---
title: "End or rejoin an interview"
description: "Understand collapsing, quitting, and ending a session before reviewing it."
---

Use **End Interview** when your practice or interview is complete to finish the session and proceed to its record and report.

## End a session

1. Select **Interview duration** in the toolbar.
2. Choose **End Interview**.
3. Follow the prompts and wait to return to the workspace.
4. Check its status in **Interview Records**, then select **View Report**.

<Frame caption="Choose End Interview when the session is complete.">
  <img src="/images/client/end-session.png" alt="Interview duration menu showing separate Quit App and End Interview actions." width="240" />
</Frame>

You cannot rejoin an ended session. Return Home and launch a new one to continue practicing.

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Minimize to floating widget | Session continues; click the bubble to restore |
| Quit App | Leave the client while the interview and time consumption continue |
| End Interview | Finish the session and review it in records |

## Rejoin an ongoing session

If you only quit the app, reopen it, find the ongoing session in the workspace, and use the available rejoin action. To finish completely, select **Interview duration → End Interview** in the assistant.

Afterward, review the summary, Q&A, and transcript. Users spending credits can also open **Account & Billing → View usage history → Credit usage** to check charges.
