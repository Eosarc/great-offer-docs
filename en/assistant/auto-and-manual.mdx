Choose whether suggestions follow detected questions automatically or wait for your action.

## Automatic answering

Enable **Auto-answer** before launch or under **Menu → Auto Answer** during a session. The assistant detects questions from the conversation and shows suggestions in the answer area.

Read the conclusion as the response appears and wait for the rest. Use text chat to add conditions or request another style.

## AI Answer

1. Turn off **Auto Answer** in the assistant menu.
2. Wait for the interviewer's question in the transcript and check the context.
3. Select **AI Answer** in the toolbar.
4. Read the result in the answer area.

Transcription can continue with automatic answering off, so you can hear the full question before requesting help.

| Situation | Input |
|---|---|
| Follow questions automatically | Auto Answer |
| Listen first, then generate | AI Answer |
| Specify a question, condition, or output requirement | Chat |

For example, ask in **Chat**: “Organize this as a one-minute answer using background, actions, and results.”
