---
title: "Window controls and shortcuts"
description: "Move or hide panels, collapse the assistant, and review earlier answers."
---

Learn the controls before a session so suggestions remain easy to read.

## Show, hide, and move

- Use **Show transcript** or **Hide transcript** in the toolbar to control transcript visibility. Hiding it does not stop transcription.
- Select the answer panel's hide button to hide that panel. Responses already generating continue.
- Drag the answer panel's resize handle to change its height.
- Drag the toolbar's **Drag assistant** area to move the whole assistant window away from the call or question.

## Floating widget

Select **Minimize to floating widget**, then click the widget to restore the assistant. The session continues while collapsed. Use **Menu → Open Dashboard** to return to the workspace.

## Previous answers

Use the left and right controls in the answer area. The counter shows your position; unavailable navigation controls are disabled.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Check the hints beside buttons or menu items for shortcuts to screenshots, chat, collapsing the assistant, and navigating Q&A. Available shortcuts and key combinations may vary by operating system and version. You can also click the controls directly.

Use [End Interview](/en/assistant/end-session) when finished.
