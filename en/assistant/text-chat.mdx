Select **Chat** in the toolbar to open the question box. Use it to add context, request a structure, or explore a topic.

## Send a question

1. Open **Chat**.
2. Describe your task and desired output.
3. Select **Send**.
4. Read the question and response in the answer area, then send follow-ups as needed.

Use previous and next controls to review answers. After ending, open **Interview Records → View Report → Q&A**.

## Questions to try

**Structure:** “Give me three points for introducing a user activation project in a product manager interview. Provide a general framework.”

**Calculation:** “The control and treatment groups each have 1,200 people, with 372 and 564 activated. Calculate activation rates, absolute uplift, and relative uplift, and explain the difference.”

**Follow-ups:** “Give me three follow-up angles on ‘Why did you choose this solution?’ and the evidence each answer needs.”

**Documents:** “Using the document selected for this session, summarize project goals, metrics, and lessons. Mark details the material does not specify.”

Check facts before adapting suggestions to your own experience.
