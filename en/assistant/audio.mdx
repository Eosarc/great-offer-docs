---
title: "Choose audio devices"
description: "Configure interviewer audio and your own microphone separately."
---

Interviewer audio provides question context; your microphone captures your spoken answers for review.

## Set defaults before a session

1. Open **App settings → Preferences**.
2. Choose the meeting output device under **Interviewer audio**, or use **System default**.
3. Choose your input under **Microphone**, or use **System default**.
4. Confirm the meeting app's speaker and microphone match the intended devices.

## During a session

Open **Audio settings** in the assistant toolbar. Check **Interviewer audio source**, **Your microphone**, and connection information. Check again after changing headphones or external devices.

## Check both voices

In a practice call, ask your partner to say a complete question and check the transcript. Answer aloud, then review your text in **Transcript** after ending.

If needed, verify microphone, system audio, and screen permissions; confirm meeting audio plays through the selected device; check microphone mute status; and align the meeting app and assistant after connecting Bluetooth or external devices.
