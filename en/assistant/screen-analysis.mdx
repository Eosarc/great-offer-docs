---
title: "Analyze a screenshot"
description: "Submit an on-screen question, chart, or code problem for analysis."
---

Open **Screenshot Analysis** when a question appears in visual material on your screen.

## Before capturing

Check screen permissions in **App Settings → Interview Access Permissions**. Display the question with the relevant inputs, constraints, and examples.

## Select and submit

1. Select **Screenshot Analysis** in the toolbar.
2. Choose the area to analyze.
3. Adjust the selection edges and corners to keep necessary context.
4. Select **Analyze Selected Area**.
5. Return to the assistant for progress and results. Use **Chat** to add requirements.

Cancel from the selection interface if you do not want to submit.

## Keep useful context

- Code: problem statement, input/output, and constraints.
- Data: legends, axes, units, and time periods.
- Processes or systems: participants, constraints, and task requirements.
- Documents: the relevant referenced paragraphs.

Check the selection before submitting and exclude unrelated chats, account details, and private content.
