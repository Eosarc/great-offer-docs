After launch, the assistant supports audio questions, text input, and on-screen problems.

## Main areas

The **transcript area** shows recognized conversation text and can be expanded or hidden. The **answer area** shows questions and AI suggestions. While generation is in progress, content continues to appear. Use the previous and next controls to review multiple answers.

| Toolbar action | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Audio settings | Choose interviewer audio and your microphone |
| AI Answer | Generate a suggestion from current audio context |
| Screenshot | Submit an on-screen problem |
| Chat | Type and send a question |
| Interview duration | Check duration and access exit/end actions |
| Menu | Automatic answering, Stealth Mode, and workspace access |
| Minimize to floating widget | Shrink and later restore the assistant |

## Choose an input method

Use automatic answering to follow detected questions, **AI Answer** to control timing, **Chat** for explicit conditions, or **Screenshot** for visual material.

## Use suggestions

Identify the conclusion, evidence, and key figures, then speak in your own words. Check personal facts against your experience. Answer aloud during practice so you can review your delivery afterward.
