---
title: "Check the version and updates"
description: "Find the current app version and follow update prompts."
---

Open **App settings → App updates** to see your version and update status.

## Check for updates

1. Keep an internet connection active.
2. Select **Check for updates**.
3. Read the result. If an update is available, follow the app's prompts.

End an ongoing interview before restarting or installing an update.

## Help support understand your environment

When reporting a problem, include the app version, operating system version, time, and relevant session or page.

After updating, check permissions and audio devices in **App settings**. Before your next session, confirm the resume, role, answer language, and preferences.
