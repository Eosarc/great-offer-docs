---
title: "Manage app settings"
description: "Adjust interface language, default audio devices, and app display settings."
---

Open **App settings** from the workspace sidebar for preferences, permissions, updates, and feedback.

## Interface language

Open **Preferences → Interface language**. The menu offers English, Español, Français, 日本語, 한국어, and 简体中文. This changes the workspace and assistant interface. Set AI answer output separately under **Launch Copilot → Interview language**.

Some toolbar controls retain English labels, including **AI Answer**, **Screenshot**, and **Chat**.

## Default audio devices

Choose the meeting output in **Interviewer audio** and your input in **Microphone**. With **System default**, check the operating system's current defaults. Confirm again after connecting headphones. See [Audio devices](/en/assistant/audio).

## App icon display

If your client provides an option to show or hide the app icon, adjust it as needed. Hiding the icon does not close the app or end the session. Assistant visibility during sharing or recording is managed separately through [Stealth Mode](/en/privacy/stealth).

## Permissions, updates, and feedback

Check permission status for your device under **Interview access** and follow the prompts to grant access; check the version under **App updates**; send feedback under **Help and feedback**.
